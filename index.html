Operations manual

Loan process guide

Workflow, ownership, handoffs, and the checklists used throughout a loan.

01

Loan officer · Intake

Capture a complete application

Confirm the customer profile, requested product, purpose, amount, term, repayment start date, and any asset or security details.

  • Collect and attach the documents that apply to this customer and product at intake; mark an item received only when its evidence is attached.
  • Use Not applicable only when a requirement genuinely does not apply. Required product documents, including M-Pesa statements and logbook-loan evidence, must be provided.
  • Submit the application to place it in the credit review queue.
Handoff Submitted application, initial checklist statuses, and available supporting evidence.
02

Credit analyst · Assessment

Verify risk and repayment capacity

Review the submitted evidence, customer history, existing exposure, CRB information, affordability, security, insurance, and identified risks.

  • Complete every credit assessment check, enter net monthly income, existing commitments and proposed installment, and record the assessment note.
  • Request more information when evidence or answers are missing; the application returns to the loan officer for a response.
  • Before recommending a decision, resolve every credit-stage document item as received with evidence or not applicable.
Handoff Documented recommendation, review checklist, affordability figures, and assessment note go to the Committee.
03

Credit committee · Decision

Review and vote in the Committee Portal

Raphael Kioko, Patrick Ndegwa, and Elizabeth Mungai review the complete application and discuss it in the shared Committee Portal.

  • Each member records one independent vote and rationale.
  • A decision requires two matching votes, including at least one Finance member.
  • The final decision is recorded in the audit trail and an in-app notice is sent to all loan officers.
Handoff Committee-approved application moves to Admin; a declined application is closed.
04

Admin · Offer letter

Prepare and send the offer letter

Enter the approved facility terms, generate the PDF, save it to the loan file, and email it to the originating loan officer.

Handoff Only after email delivery succeeds does the application move to Operations for pre-disbursement checks.
05

Operations · Disbursement

Complete pre-disbursement checks

Confirm approval, facility terms, required security and perfection documents, insurance, customer details, and payout instructions against the recorded conditions.

  • Resolve each perfection checklist item as received with evidence or not applicable; the system blocks disbursement while items remain unresolved.
  • Confirm disbursement only when the operational checks are complete.
Handoff Confirmed disbursement starts the repayment schedule and servicing process.
06

Loan officer · Servicing and collections

Record repayments and manage arrears

Monitor installments after disbursement, record actual customer payments against the right loan and installment, and follow up overdue amounts.

  • Use the repayment register for received amounts and references; distinguish a part payment from a settled installment.
  • Review overdue loans and installments, document follow-up, and escalate collection concerns under the applicable policy.
Ongoing control Keep payment history and customer communication current through settlement or closure.

Apply the current approved credit, delegated authority, security, disbursement, and collections policies whenever they set a stricter requirement.

Loan file documents

At intake, use the application checklist to record status and attach evidence for documents currently required. Review recorded statuses and files from the loan record after submission.

Credit assessment

These checks are completed by the analyst before recommending a decision.

    Decision and handoff gates

    • Analyst recommendation has complete checks, affordability figures, and a written assessment.
    • Two matching committee votes, including one Finance vote, are recorded before a decision is final.
    • Admin saves the offer PDF and emails it to the loan officer before routing the application to Operations.
    • Loan officers receive an in-app notice and can open the offer PDF from the application.
    • Operations confirms approval conditions and required documents before disbursement.
    • Repayments and collections activity are recorded against the correct loan.
